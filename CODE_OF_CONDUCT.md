# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work rather than the person,
and help make this a welcoming place for people of every background and level of experience.
Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers through a
[private advisory](https://github.com/weizyyy/Agentic-Meeting/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

---

# 行为准则

本项目采用[贡献者公约 2.1 版](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)。

简而言之：相互尊重，善意理解他人，就事论事，共同营造对不同背景、不同经验的参与者都友好的环境。
骚扰和人身攻击不被容忍。

如遇不当行为，请通过[私密公告](https://github.com/weizyyy/Agentic-Meeting/security/advisories/new)
或直接联系维护者进行报告，报告内容将被保密处理。
